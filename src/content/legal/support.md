# Support

**Last updated: 16 October 2026**

Help with Wobbi. For anything not answered here write to hello@wobbi.app.

## If you need help right now

If you might be in danger, call your local emergency number. Free, confidential lines:

- United States & Canada: call or text 988
- United Kingdom & Ireland: Samaritans 116 123
- Australia: Lifeline 13 11 14
- Other countries: findahelpline.com

## Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your device without one. An account only adds sync between devices.

## How do I delete my data?

On the device: delete the app. In your account: More → Account → Delete account. That permanently removes your account, entries, photos and voice memos.

## How do I export my entries?

More → Export data. You get a JSON file of your entries (photos and audio are not included).

## Apple Health

Turn it on or off in More → Apple Health. You can also manage access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Wobbi.

## Reminders are not arriving

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Wobbi is allowed, and that the reminder is on in More → Reminders. Reminders are skipped for today once you have already done the thing.

## Is my AI key safe?

The key is stored only on your device and used only when you tap an AI action. It is never sent to us.
